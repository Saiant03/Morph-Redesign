## Cross-brand patterns [F, then I]

1. **Story before spec**: Byredo, Le Labo, Diptyque, D.S. & Durga, Matière Première and Crivelli all do this. The scannable note list stays close by.
2. **Top/heart/base** is the standard note format. Crivelli's contrast formula is the exception.
3. **Trial is a first-class category** with its own nav entry at almost every niche house.
4. **Credit-back on discovery sets** appears at about half of the brands (Le Labo, Crivelli, JHAG, Phlur). It is a differentiator, not a baseline.
5. **Simple family taxonomies** (4 families at Diptyque and D.S. & Durga) lead discovery. Deep facets are secondary or absent.
6. **Cross-sell by the same scent** (hair mist, body lotion) rather than "you may also like".
7. **Neutral UI with the product as hero**. The exceptions (Diptyque botanicals, D.S. & Durga maximalism, JHAG ornament) are the memorable ones.
8. **Free-shipping thresholds** everywhere. No house ships free unconditionally.

## Where Morph already stands out [I]

- Deeper attribute data than most references (notes by tier, season, occasion, intensity, longevity, style, family).
- A **working** weighted quiz (Frama's "finder" is not one).
- A physical, blind layering product. Only Jo Malone and Escentric treat layering as a system, and neither has a blind reveal mechanic.
- A strong, colorful bottle (most references are clear or neutral glass).
- A physical boutique with praised staff, plus Certilogo authentication.

## Principles to adopt (with reasons)

1. **Families first, facets second.** A small, customer-worded family set (4–6) as the first click; notes move to search and autocomplete. Reason: Morph's 14+ flat families and 50-value note lists create choice overload.
2. **A one-line scent descriptor on every card** (D.S. & Durga) or a contrast line (Crivelli). Reason: comparison without opening PDPs.
3. **Try-first on the PDP** (Diptyque): the travel 2×8 ml or a sample right under the 100 ml. Reason: a blind purchase of 690–790 lei online.
4. **Credit-back discovery set**: to be evaluated with Morph's margins. Reason: it removes the risk of the full-size purchase and gives the discovery set a clear job.
5. **Pairings as named, linkable entities** (Jo Malone), plus "combiner" suggestions. Reason: SEO, sharing, and giving layering a place outside the blind box.
6. **Same-scent ritual cross-sell** (gel, cream, room spray of the same scent). Morph already has the range.
7. **Reserve or consult in store** (Diptyque's "Reserve in store"). Reason: Casa Morph reviews show the consultation converts.
8. **Classification tags at the top of the PDP** (Byredo): family · intensity · longevity from the existing attributes.

## Do not copy

- Byredo's missing reviews and cross-sell: Morph's value is helping people decide.
- Frama's fake finder.
- Matière Première's three disconnected taxonomies.
- Le Labo's hidden sample price: the Romanian market is price-aware.
- Aesop-style notes only in prose.
- Stacked gift-with-purchase thresholds without margin logic.
- Any competitor's visual identity (Le Labo's apothecary style, Byredo's minimal style, Diptyque's botanical illustration).
