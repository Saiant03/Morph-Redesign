# 05 — Opportunity map

Built on 01–04. Evidence markers point to those files. Everything here is **interpretation and recommendation**, not fact.

## The biggest opportunity

**Morph already owns every part of a best-in-class discovery system, but the parts don't connect.** The attribute data, the weighted quiz, travel 2×8 ml sizes, sample sets, blind layering sets, the Casa Morph consultation, loyalty points and Certilogo all exist (01 §2, 8–10, 18). Online they are separate dead ends, and the store sells bottles as interchangeable SKUs. The brand's promise ("Identity, layer by layer", "Your Next Form", synesthesia) is about finding and changing your scent identity.

The redesign should turn this into one continuous journey, the digital form of what the boutique staff already do in person:

```
FIND  →  TRY  →  OWN  →  LAYER  →  (next form)
quiz,     travel/     100 ml +      pairings,      loyalty,
families, sample,     ritual        composer,      new drops
filters   credit      (gel, cream)  blind reveal
```

Every screen should know where the visitor is in that loop and offer the next step.

## What is missing

1. **A visible trial path.** For a 690–790 lei blind purchase online, trial is the main lever. It is currently under "Seturi", absent from PDPs and partly sold out.
2. **Layering online.** It exists only as a boxed product. There are no pairings on PDPs, no way to combine owned scents, and no link from a set to its components.
3. **Explanation.** Quiz results, cards and PDP headers don't say *why* a scent fits or how it wears, although the data exists.
4. **A brand world in images and motion.** Synesthesia, Naples and the Bormioli bottle live only in paragraphs.
5. **Designed mobile.** Today's mobile is a stacked desktop: price and CTA below the fold, ≈12k px homepage.
6. **Social proof per product.** 0 reviews on 105 products; reviews exist only for the store.
7. **One source of truth.** Concentrations, collection counts and set contents contradict each other.

## Morph × metamorphosis × identity × scent × transformation × layering

**Does this territory have substance?** Yes, but only where it rests on product truths, and those exist:

- *Morph* (Greek *morphē*, form) plus the twisted bottle "equilibrium and continuous movement": the form idea is in the object itself.
- *Scent transforms*: a perfume literally changes on skin over hours (top → base). Morph's concentration and longevity claims make this central.
- *Layering transforms*: two scents make a third. The line is literally called "Your Next Form".
- *Identity*: unisex, "a scent that represents you" (the quiz headline), "Identity, layer by layer".
- *Synesthesia*: the founding idea (scent = color = emotion), with visible evidence in the juice colors.

**Where it becomes a cliché:** butterflies and chrysalises, liquid "morphing" blobs (generic WebGL goo), self-help "transform yourself" copy, glitch-style shape-shifting.

**Recommendation:** use transformation as a **behavioral principle** (the interface changes state in response to choices and over time: a color blends, a timeline advances, a box opens) and use **synesthetic color** as the **visual principle**. Do not illustrate metamorphosis literally. Of the two, synesthesia is the more distinctive and defensible territory, because Morph can show it (the bottles), while "transformation" alone is claimed by many beauty brands.

## What makes it distinctly Morph, not "another luxury fragrance site"

1. **Color as information.** Each scent's real juice color is its identity in the UI. Families and pairings are shown as color relationships. No reference brand does this; most use clear glass and neutral UIs.
2. **Layering as a first-class system** (composer, pairing pages, blind reveal), not a blog topic.
3. **Time as structure.** Scents are presented as they wear, not as a static pyramid.
4. **Italian craft, local ritual.** The Naples origin and Bormioli glass, plus Casa Morph București (people, consultation, booking).
5. **Explained recommendations.** Every suggestion says why (the quiz tags and attributes make this cheap).

## Constraints to respect

- SEO equity: about 90 RO/EN posts, bilingual URLs and a large body of category text (Rank Math). The redesign must keep the URLs or redirect them, and move SEO text rather than delete it.
- Operations: RO couriers and COD, invoicing, loyalty balances and Certilogo must survive whatever platform is chosen.
- Photography: the catalog has packshots on white. Signature ideas must work with the existing assets plus a realistic new shoot (juices, ingredients, the boutique).
